# MoneyFest

**Money + Fest = Manifest.** Manifesting debt-free, one payday at a time.

A personal budget planner that shows income, expenses and what's left every month.

- Works with any pay schedule: twice a month (15th & 30th), weekly, or monthly
- Monthly, weekly, one-time and installment expenses
- Debt-free countdown, Paid tracker with overdue checks, yearly dashboard
- Shopping tally that reads prices from a photo (in your browser, never uploaded)
- Live currency converter

Your data is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is stored in this repo. Use **Backup** regularly.
